Featured workshop

Building a Four-Year High School Plan

A practical session for students and parents who want to make purposeful course choices without losing sight of balance, interests, and long-term goals.

You will learn how to

  • Connect graduation requirements with future goals
  • Think wisely about rigor, electives, and balance
  • Use activities to explore interests and develop leadership
  • Identify the next decisions your family needs to make
